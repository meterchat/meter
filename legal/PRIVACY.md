# Meter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-23

This Privacy Policy explains how Meter (“Meter”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our products and services (the “Services”).

This document is a product-friendly policy intended to be clear and practical. It is not legal advice. If you need a policy tailored to a specific jurisdiction, consult counsel.

## 1) What Meter does

Meter is a pay‑per‑thought AI product for builders. Meter can route requests to multiple AI model providers, run structured debates, and generate artifacts (e.g., architecture docs, requirements, agent configuration files) that can be pushed to your GitHub repository.

Depending on what you connect, Meter may also access external services (e.g., GitHub, Google/Gmail) to perform actions you request.

## 2) Information we collect

### A. Information you provide

Account information. If you create an account, we collect information such as your name, email address, and authentication identifiers.

Content you submit. We collect the prompts, messages, uploaded files, debate configurations, and other inputs you provide to the Services (“User Content”), as well as outputs generated by the Services.

Billing information. If you pay for Meter, we collect billing details such as invoice information, payment method tokens, and transaction metadata. Payment card information is generally processed by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.

### B. Information from connected services

If you connect third‑party services, we may collect data you authorize via that integration. Examples:

GitHub. Repository metadata, installation identifiers, selected repository access, commit metadata, and files we create or modify at your direction.

Google (including Gmail, if enabled). OAuth profile information and the minimum email metadata/content necessary to provide the feature you enable.

We only request scopes needed for a feature, and we only access the repositories/accounts you explicitly authorize.

### C. Automatically collected information

Usage data. Events such as page views, clicks, feature usage, time stamps, and session identifiers.

Device and log data. IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and diagnostic logs.

Cookies/local storage. We use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, preferences, and analytics.

## 3) How we use information

We use information to:

Provide and operate the Services, including routing model requests, running debates, and generating artifacts.

Authenticate users and secure accounts.

Process payments, prevent fraud, and maintain accurate billing.

Maintain and improve the Services, including debugging and performance.

Provide support and communicate with you about updates.

Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

## 4) How AI model providers receive data

Meter can send portions of your User Content to third‑party AI model providers when you request an AI response. Those providers process the content to return outputs.

What is sent. Typically this includes your prompt, relevant context you choose to include (including prior messages and attached materials), and system instructions required to run the requested workflow.

Provider policies. Each provider’s handling of data is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Minimization. We aim to send only the information necessary to fulfill your request.

## 5) How we share information

We may share information with:

Service providers. Vendors that help us run the Services (hosting, logging, analytics, payment processing, support tools). They may access data only to perform services for us.

AI model providers. To generate responses.

Connected services. To perform actions you request (e.g., committing artifacts to GitHub).

Legal and safety. If we believe disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 6) Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the Services and for legitimate business purposes (such as auditing, security, dispute resolution, and compliance).

You may delete certain data from within the product when available, or request deletion as described below.

## 7) Your choices and controls

Account controls. You can update certain account information in the product.

Disconnect integrations. You can revoke access to connected services (e.g., GitHub App, Google OAuth) at any time in the third‑party provider’s settings.

Marketing preferences. You can opt out of marketing emails by following unsubscribe instructions.

Access and deletion. You may request access to or deletion of personal information by contacting us.

## 8) Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9) International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. We take steps designed to ensure appropriate safeguards when transferring data.

## 10) Children

The Services are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 11) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date and, if changes are material, provide additional notice.
